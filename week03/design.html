Design Principles

Visual Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy is the arrangement or presentation of elements in a way that implies importance. In perspective user interface design, visual hierarchy aims to guide the user's attention to important actions or pieces of information first.

An effective visual hierarchy helps users understand the relative importance of different elements on a page.
