Money guidance for your first real paycheck.

No jargon, no shame about starting late, no assumption you already know how any of this works. The First Paycheck breaks budgeting, saving, and debt down into plain steps, then gives you a calculator that turns your own numbers into a plan.

Built for people just starting out

Most personal finance advice is written for people who already have savings, a mortgage, and a 401(k) match to think about. This site is written for the paycheck before all of that — your first one, or one of your first few.

A budget that survives contact with real life

Learn a simple way to split your paycheck into needs, wants, and savings, without a spreadsheet you'll abandon by week three.

Saving before it feels urgent

Small, automatic habits that build a cushion before an emergency forces the issue.

Debt you can actually reason about

A clear way to tell the difference between debt that's working for you and debt that's working against you.

See where your money actually goes

Enter your income and a few expenses into the calculator and get an honest read on your leftover balance and savings rate — saved on your device so it's there next time you check in.
